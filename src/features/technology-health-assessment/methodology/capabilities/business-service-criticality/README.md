# Business Service Criticality

This capability identifies and prioritizes the services whose disruption would materially affect the business.

Primary report category:
- Governance and Risk Management

Secondary relationships:
- Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
- Technology Operations

Readiness: Pilot. This is a foundational capability because recovery, security, and technology investment decisions need a shared view of business importance. Unknown answers create visibility findings; they do not assert that a critical service is absent.
