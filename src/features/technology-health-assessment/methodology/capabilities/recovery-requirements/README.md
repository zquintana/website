# Recovery Requirements

This capability translates business priorities into measurable recovery time, data-loss, and validation requirements.

Primary report category:
- Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

Secondary relationships:
- Governance and Risk Management
- Technology Operations

Readiness: Pilot. Business Service Criticality identifies which services matter; this capability defines and validates the recovery requirements for those services. Unknown answers create visibility findings and do not assert that recovery is impossible.
