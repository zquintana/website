# Scope and non-goals

The business direction is defined in [Business goals](business-goals.md). This page separates that long-term direction from what the current implementation actually delivers. The existing deep assessment is currently disabled as a public entry point; see [assessment track status](assessment-track-status.md).

## Current scope

- The consulting site and direct consultation path.
- A preserved, but currently disabled, in-depth assessment implementation under `src/features/technology-health-assessment/`.
- A future simpler executive assessment is planned but not yet implemented.

## Current non-goals

The product does not certify regulatory compliance, guarantee security, replace a penetration test, replace legal advice, provide reliable industry benchmarks, automatically validate every self-reported answer, support every industry or business size equally, act as a generalized rules engine, provide a full GRC platform, act as an MSP remote-management product, or provide an approved assessment editor. Vendor products are not the primary methodology structure.

It also does not currently provide the simpler replacement assessment, automated technical verification, evidence file storage, server persistence, historical migration, a typed report API, continuous monitoring, trustworthy benchmarking, or a finalized standards interpretation.

## Future possibilities, not commitments

Future work may add service-criticality interviews, evidence workflows, integrations, benchmark populations, an authoring tool, server persistence, and richer exports. Each requires an explicit architecture and methodology decision before implementation.
