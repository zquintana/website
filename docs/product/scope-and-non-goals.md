# Scope and non-goals

The business direction is defined in [Business goals](business-goals.md). This page separates that long-term direction from what the current implementation actually delivers.

## Current scope

- A browser-based Astro assessment at `/assessment`.
- Required business and respondent profile fields plus optional context.
- Ten weighted report categories totaling 100.
- Twenty-three questions: eight migrated capability questions and 15 legacy demonstration questions.
- Preliminary 0–100 health scoring, maturity labels, confidence averages, findings, recommendations, strengths, printable results, and a consultation mailto link.
- Local-only save/resume through one localStorage record.
- A versioned modular methodology manifest with a read-model adapter for the current flat UI.

## Current non-goals

The product does not certify regulatory compliance, guarantee security, replace a penetration test, replace legal advice, provide reliable industry benchmarks, automatically validate every self-reported answer, support every industry or business size equally, act as a generalized rules engine, provide a full GRC platform, act as an MSP remote-management product, or provide an approved assessment editor. Vendor products are not the primary methodology structure.

It also does not currently provide a complete applicability engine, automated technical verification, evidence file storage, server persistence, historical migration, a typed report API, continuous monitoring, trustworthy benchmarking, or a finalized standards interpretation.

## Future possibilities, not commitments

Future work may add service-criticality interviews, evidence workflows, integrations, benchmark populations, an authoring tool, server persistence, and richer exports. Each requires an explicit architecture and methodology decision before implementation.
