# Assessment track status

The current capability-based assessment is temporarily disabled as a public product entry point.

It remains in the repository as a future in-depth advisory track under `src/features/technology-health-assessment/`. Its methodology, capability modules, scoring, findings, persistence, and reports are preserved for later use and continued architectural reference. The `/assessment/*` routes currently show a deferred-track notice and are marked `noindex`.

The current product direction is a simpler executive-first assessment focused on qualified consulting conversations. That experience has not been implemented yet. Do not describe the preserved deep track as the current customer offering, and do not begin expanding its capability catalog until the simpler assessment scope is approved.

Related planning: [business goals](business-goals.md), [scope and non-goals](scope-and-non-goals.md), and the [assessment platform roadmap](../development/assessment-platform-roadmap.md).
