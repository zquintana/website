# 0001: Capability-based methodology

- Status: Accepted for the demonstration architecture
- Date: 2026-07-27

## Context

Categories are useful for reporting but are too broad and presentation-oriented to own a durable methodology.

## Decision

Capabilities own coherent outcomes, questions, evidence guidance, findings, recommendations, and mappings. Domains organize capabilities.

## Alternatives considered

Vendor product checklists, category-owned questions, and a generalized rules engine.

## Consequences

Modules are independently reviewable and vendor-neutral. The read-model adapter remains necessary until the UI consumes capabilities directly.
