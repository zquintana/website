# 0003: Separate health, risk, and confidence

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-27

## Context

A weak capability, a severe business consequence, and weak evidence are different facts.

## Decision

Keep maturity/health, findings/risk, and evidence confidence as separate values and report them separately.

## Consequences

The report can express high health with low confidence and provisional risk without overstating certainty.
