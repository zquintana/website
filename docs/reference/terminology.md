# Terminology

- Assessment: one respondent session producing preliminary technology-health results.
- Assessment version: a versioned set of taxonomy, questions, scoring, findings, recommendations, and mappings.
- Domain: stable taxonomy grouping of capabilities.
- Report category: customer-facing aggregation/presentation grouping with a weight.
- Capability: primary methodology unit with a business outcome and owned content.
- Foundational capability: prerequisite or high-leverage capability marker; currently metadata only.
- Control: specific safeguard or practice tested by one or more questions.
- Question: respondent-facing prompt that tests an observable concept.
- Answer option: selectable response with optional maturity score.
- Applicability: whether a capability/question belongs in the assessment scope.
- Evidence: support for an answer, from respondent claim through verification.
- Health: implementation/maturity result, 0–5 or normalized 0–100.
- Maturity: ordered health scale; current labels range from Not Present to Optimized.
- Risk: business consequence/likelihood treatment associated with a condition.
- Finding: a risk, visibility gap, or improvement condition.
- Critical finding: finding meeting a documented severe-impact threshold; not merely an unanswered question.
- Visibility finding: uncertainty or lack of evidence that warrants follow-up.
- Confidence: strength of evidence supporting a result.
- Recommendation: action, expected outcome, and optional timing/effort.
- Standards mapping: relationship between a capability/recommendation and a framework reference.
- Provisional score: score produced under uncertainty using question-specific behavior.
- Legacy question: unmigrated MVP question exposed through the flat read model.
- Active methodology version: version selected by `methodology/index.ts` for current runtime assembly.
